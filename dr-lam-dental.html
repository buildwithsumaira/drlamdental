Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need before your first visit to Dr. Lam's practice.

Do you accept walk-in patients? +
Yes! Walk-ins are always welcome at Dr. Lam's practice during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM). No referral or pre-booking required.
What insurance plans do you accept? +
We accept most major dental insurance plans. We also offer very affordable self-pay pricing — call us at (212) 766-2128 to verify your specific coverage before your visit.
Is Dr. Lam accepting new patients? +
Absolutely. Dr. Lam is actively welcoming new patients from all backgrounds. We treat students, families, seniors, and professionals — everyone is welcome at our Chinatown practice.
How affordable is treatment at this practice? +
Dr. Lam is well known across NYC for providing premium-quality dental care at prices that are genuinely accessible. Hundreds of patients — including students and budget-conscious New Yorkers — recommend the practice specifically for its value.
Where exactly is the office located? +
We're located at 198 Canal St, Suite 402, New York, NY 10013 — in the heart of Chinatown. We're steps from the Canal St subway station (N, Q, R, W, J, Z, 6 trains), making us easily accessible from any borough.
